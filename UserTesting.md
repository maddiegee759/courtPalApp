# User Testing
## Tasks and questions:
### Tasks
Record whether each task was completed: independently, with a hint, not at all
1. Create an account and sign in
2. Add friend account and find their recent tennis activity
3. You are playing a tennis match. Record the result, attach a photograph and find the saved match
4. You want to arrange a future match. Schedule one using the calendar
5. Record a training session, then find where it's saved
6. You want to turn on dark mode. Find the setting and enable it

### Questions
1. On a scale of 1–5, how easy was courtPal to use?
2. Which part was most confusing?
3. What is one thing you would improve?
